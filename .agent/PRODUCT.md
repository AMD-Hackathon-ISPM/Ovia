# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Ovia serves adults aged 18 or older, with at least one ovary and not currently pregnant, answering a structured questionnaire about themselves and optionally uploading a pelvic ultrasound image they already have. They need plain-language results that avoid false reassurance and a clear next step. Demo operators need a complete, resettable synthetic-data flow. Reviewers need visible model scope, calibration status, evidence boundaries, and limitations.

## Product Purpose

Ovia is an investigational screening-support prototype. It pairs a structured questionnaire with an optional de-identified pelvic ultrasound still and returns three independent risk assessments: PCOS, ovarian cyst, and ovarian tumor. It does not diagnose, identify, or rule out any of them, and it does not assess malignancy. Every participant is directed to confirmatory evaluation regardless of any result.

Success means the flow is understandable on a phone, honest about uncertainty, transient with personal data, bilingual in English and Bahasa Indonesia, and unambiguous about the next step.

## Brand Personality

Soft, calm, and unhurried, without ever trading honesty for comfort. Warm enough that a person answering questions about their own body is not alarmed; disciplined enough that softness is never spent on making a result feel safer than it is.

## Anti-references

- Interfaces that imply certainty, clearance, or a diagnosis.
- Celebratory green states, traffic-light scales, checkmarks, or reassuring motion for a lower band.
- Exclusion screens that read as a judgement about the person rather than a limit of the tool.
- Decoration that exists only to soften; every soft choice must still carry information.
- Futuristic medical-scan aesthetics, scanning lines, neon overlays, decorative glass.
- Dense research dashboards that make an operator interpret model internals before finding the next step.
- Marketing-led health interfaces that hide uncertainty, cohort limits, or limitations.
- Any single combined score, and any green or checkmark on a low assessment.

## Design Principles

1. Make the mandatory next step impossible to miss, structurally.
2. Expose uncertainty without making users interpret research metadata.
3. Keep the three screenings visibly separate and equal; never let the layout imply they combine or rank.
4. Use progressive disclosure for reviewer detail while keeping the operator-facing result concise.
5. Treat ineligibility as a boundary, not a rejection.
6. Design phone-first, with every critical state usable on low-end devices and intermittent networks.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Preserve critical copy and controls at 320 CSS pixels and 200% zoom. Touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels, keyboard-operable controls, visible focus, accessible names and text alternatives (including for the sonogram and any overlay), reduced-motion behavior, and redundant non-color status cues. English and Bahasa Indonesia must remain equivalent, human-reviewed, and layout-safe.
